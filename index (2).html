The Club

Built from the ground up.

Ground Up Jiu Jitsu is Elland's home for Brazilian Jiu Jitsu — a place to learn real technique, get in shape, and become part of a team that shows up for each other. Whether you're stepping on the mat for the first time or you're chasing competition podiums, the approach is the same: strong fundamentals, honest coaching, and a room that pushes you without ego.

Training is built around live rolling and position-specific drilling, so what you learn in class actually holds up when it counts.

Head Coach · 2nd Degree Black Belt

Rob Barbiero

A 2nd degree black belt, Rob leads every fundamentals and advanced session at Ground Up, with a coaching style built on clear, methodical instruction and plenty of mat time. Focused on giving beginners a genuinely welcoming first class and giving experienced grapplers a reason to keep sharpening their game.
